## Setting in One Paragraph

The game takes place roughly **2,000 years after a civilizational collapse** caused by an ancient
civilization's dependence on a resource now called "Black Matter" (what players would recognize as
fossil fuels). The continent that survived — **Lost Angeles** — is a crescent-shaped landmass formed
from fractured remnants of old-world geography. Civilization persists inside **Stable Zones**
maintained by **Ether crystals**, a naturally occurring energy source that also acts as a reality
stabilizer. At the edges of these zones lies **The Mist** — not weather, but a zone of Etheric
distortion, mutation, and ruin that slowly encroaches on the living world.

## World Factions (Lore)

Five civilizations survive on the continent, each a distinct response to the same historical trauma.
They do not merely compete for territory — they compete over **how humanity should survive inside a
damaged world**.

| Faction | Core survival thesis | Lore reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Linh Sơn** | Preserve balance — strength bound to reverence | [`faction-bible-v2.md §1`](../lore/faction-bible-v2.md) |
| **Đế Quốc** (Republic Empire) | Impose order — rebuild civilization through industrial might | [`faction-bible-v2.md §2`](../lore/faction-bible-v2.md) |
| **Thiên Lữ** | Carry living memory — nomadic keepers of Etheric knowledge | [`faction-bible-v2.md §3`](../lore/faction-bible-v2.md) |

## Geography Overview

The continent **Lost Angeles** is divided into regions that carry geological memory of the old world.
Key design properties:

- Mountain chains form a broken spine — Linh Sơn territory.
- Coastal arcs and reef-choked lowlands — Đế Quốc industrial zones.
- Open steppe and sky-corridor routes — Thiên Lữ migration paths.
- Each region is simultaneously a physical place, cultural space, historical layer, and mission zone.

*Full regional detail: [`../lore/region-bible-v1.md`](../lore/region-bible-v1.md)*

## Tone & Presentation

- **Not grimdark, not utopian.** The world is damaged but inhabited with dignity.
- No faction is morally pure — each holds one true thing and misunderstands another.
- Lore is **discovered through play** (mission flavour, NPC dialogue, room lore snippets), not
  front-loaded in tutorials.
- Vietnamese proper nouns (Linh Sơn, Đế Quốc, Thiên Lữ, Sơn Thể, etc.) are preserved verbatim.
  All player-facing UI copy is English.
