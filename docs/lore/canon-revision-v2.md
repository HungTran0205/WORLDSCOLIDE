# WORLDSCOLIDE — Canon Revision v2

> Internal canon consolidation draft for lore, narrative, art direction, and future quest design.
> This version integrates world core revisions, layered historical timeline, Ether weakening, the shared origin of Thiên Lữ and The Eliza, and the moral ambiguity of Donny Ivan.

---

# 1. Core World Thesis

WORLDSCOLIDE is not simply a post-apocalyptic world.
It is a world where humanity survives inside the **remaining stable zones of reality**, protected by Ether, while beyond those zones lies the long, mutated aftermath of an ancient civilizational decision.

The people of the current age do not live in the full world that once existed.
They live in what remains of it.

At the center of this reality lies one terrible truth:

> The current world was not created by a god.
> It was shaped by a human decision so vast and violent that later civilizations mistook its consequences for divine creation.

---

# 2. The Age Before Ether

Before the current era, there existed a civilization that modern people only remember through fragments, myths, and corrupted terminology.

No one in the current age truly understands the concept of **oil**.
They do not use that word.
Instead, they speak of an ancient material known in myths and distorted records as:

- **Black Matter**
- **The Dark Substance**
- **Earth’s Black Blood**
- **Night Resin**
- **The Buried Liquid Fire**

According to scattered legends, the civilization before Ether built its power on this black substance drawn from deep beneath the earth.
It fueled machines, cities, war, and expansion.
It also became inseparable from the great destruction that ended that age.

For the people of the current world, the truth is simple:

> Before the Ether Age, there was another age.
> It was built on Black Matter.
> That age burned itself to death.

Whether deposits of that substance still exist somewhere beyond The Mist is unknown.
Some late-game theories suggest that outside the stable Ether zones, remnants of the old Black Matter civilization may still survive in twisted forms.

# 3. Ether Crystals

## 3.1 Public Understanding

To the civilizations of the current era, Ether is:
- a source of power
- a sacred or strategic resource
- a force tied to awakening, mutation, memory, and reality
- the foundation of survival on the continent

## 3.2 Secret Truth

Ether Crystals were first discovered and studied by **Dr. Minh Yun**, a scientist of the old world.
These crystals possessed extraordinary energy storage properties, to the point of rivaling compact nuclear-level output in practical civilization terms.

Long-term exposure to Ether radiation triggered unusual mutations in some humans, leading to the first emergence of **The Awakened**.

## 3.3 Canon Revision: Ether as Stabilizer

Ether is not merely a fuel source.
It is one of the reasons the continent remains habitable.

Ether-rich regions form **Stable Zones** — areas where reality remains coherent enough for human civilization to survive.
Where Ether is strong, the land resists full encroachment by The Mist.
Where Ether thins, distortions begin.

### Ether’s Four Functions

1. **Energy Source** — powers machines, relic systems, infrastructure, and weapons.
2. **Biological Catalyst** — influences mutation, awakening, and adaptation.
3. **Spiritual Medium** — allows rituals, visions, long-memory traditions, and contact with altered phenomena.
4. **Reality Stabilizer** — helps maintain habitable zones against the outer corruption of The Mist.

This means Ether conflict is not just an energy crisis.
It is a civilizational survival crisis.

# 4. The Mist

## 4.1 Public Myth

Every civilization has its own explanation for The Mist:
- Linh Sơn says it is the breath of ancient sea dragons.
- Thiên Lữ says it is the boundary between the world of the living and the dead.
- Đế Quốc says it is a phenomenon that can be measured and conquered.
- The Eliza says it is the veil placed by holy authority between the faithful and the outer darkness.
- Scavengers treat it as both a graveyard and a frontier.

## 4.2 Secret Truth

The Mist originated from the ancient cataclysm: fire, radiation, atmospheric collapse, chemical ruin, and the destabilizing interaction between polluted oceans and Ether-bearing strata.
Over centuries, it evolved into something more than weather and more than contamination.

It became a **boundary condition of a damaged world**.

## 4.3 Canon Lock: The Mist Has Three Layers

To prevent inconsistency, The Mist should always be understood through three interacting layers:

### Layer 1 — Physical Ruin
- toxic atmospheric remnants
- radiation scars
- thermal anomalies
- navigational interference
- poisoned coastlines and sea weather instability

### Layer 2 — Etheric Distortion
- directional corruption
- memory disruption
- perception warping
- time-sense irregularities
- unstable zones where reality seems weakened or bent

### Layer 3 — Biological and Metaphysical Intrusion
- mutated sea life
- altered predators
- entities shaped by long exposure to outer conditions
- ancient things rising through ruins, trenches, and subterranean breaks

### Canon Statement

> The Mist does not merely hide the sea.
> It distorts every system by which humans understand the world: sight, direction, memory, machinery, and time.

# 5. Donny Ivan — The Golden Face

## 5.1 Canon Position

Donny Ivan is **not** a god.
He did **not** create the world in the sense The Eliza believes.
But in a terrible literal sense, he did create the current reality.

His actions transformed the trajectory of human civilization so completely that later ages mistook historical violence for cosmic authorship.

## 5.2 Narrative Intent

Ivan should never be written as a flat villain.
He must remain morally unstable in the eyes of the audience.
Different observers should be able to reach different conclusions about him.

To some, he was:
- a decisive statesman
- a ruthless patriot
- a leader willing to do the unforgivable for the survival of his people
- the architect of a brutal but coherent future

To others, he was:
- a mad autocrat
- a mass murderer on a civilizational scale
- a tyrant who mistook power for necessity
- the man who forced the world into a new age through terror

This ambiguity is essential.

## 5.3 Canon Character Reading

Ivan wanted what he believed was best for his nation and for the future shaped under his control.
He was not trying to become a god.
He was trying to become the decisive hand of history.

But the scale of his actions exceeded politics.
Once his decisions reshaped the world itself, later generations no longer remembered him as a leader.
They remembered him as a creator, a destroyer, a face in the fire, a source of order after the end.

### Canon Statement

> Ivan was not divine.
> But history became so bent around his decisions that later civilizations mistook authorship of an age for authorship of a world.

---

# 6. Dr. Minh Yun — The Buried Counterweight

Dr. Minh Yun should function as the invisible counterweight to Donny Ivan.

If Ivan is the man who bent history,
Minh Yun is the one who first touched the truth that history later corrupted.

## 6.1 Narrative Role

Minh Yun is:
- the discoverer of Ether Crystals
- the first interpreter of their danger and promise
- a missing conscience at the root of the world
- the “ghost of truth” scattered across civilizations

## 6.2 Canon Implementation

His presence should appear across the world in fragmented forms:
- unnamed engineering principles in Đế Quốc archives
- oral traces in Linh Sơn myths
- coded references in Thiên Lữ songs
- censored passages in Eliza scripture vaults
- relic fragments and signatures circulating through Scavenger markets

He should not be fully remembered by any one faction.
Each faction holds only a broken portion of his legacy.

### Canon Statement

> No civilization remembers Minh Yun completely.
> But the world still bears fingerprints that do not belong to gods, kings, or saints — only to someone who understood the light before others learned to worship or weaponize it.

# 7. Layered History of the World

The 2000-year timespan remains canon.
To support that scale, history must be divided into multiple eras.

## Era 0 — The Great Collapse

- Ether Crystals are discovered by Dr. Minh Yun.
- Ether-based technology emerges.
- The first Awakened appear through prolonged Ether exposure.
- Donny Ivan rises as a dominant political and strategic figure.
- In pursuit of a radical break from the old world of Black Matter, Ivan unleashes a catastrophic campaign that destroys the foundations of the petroleum age.
- The oceans, atmosphere, and global infrastructure are shattered.
- The Mist is born from the long aftermath.
- Survival enclaves and bunker systems are established.

## Era 1 — The Ash Centuries

- Humanity survives in fractured enclaves.
- Ether-rich zones become the centers of habitable life.
- The earliest forms of Stable Zone civilization emerge.
- Primitive worship begins around sites of survival, artificial light, sealed shelters, preserved technology, and the image later associated with Golden Face.
- Early Ash cults form near the future site of Lady Ivan.

## Era 2 — The Era of Roads, Sky, and First Doctrine

- Trade routes, processions, proto-rail systems, and migrating knowledge networks begin linking enclaves.
- Ether reading, ritualized observation, stellar mapping, and memory traditions develop.
- A common spiritual-intellectual tradition emerges among those trying to understand Ether through sky, ritual, journey, and preserved fragments.

### The Shared Root of Thiên Lữ and The Eliza

At this stage, the ancestors of **Thiên Lữ** and **The Eliza** were not yet separate civilizations.
They were part of one broader tradition centered on interpreting light, Ether, memory, and celestial patterns.

This proto-tradition believed:
- Ether could be read through the sky and the land
- memory must be preserved through ritual forms
- civilization needed both guidance and meaning after the Collapse

### The Great Schism

A major Etheric-celestial event, remembered in different traditions under different names, triggered the divide. A working canonical title may be:

- **The Night of Seven Vaults**
- **The Sevenfold Sky Fracture**
- **The Great Revelation of the Veiled Heavens**

The event produced conflicting interpretations:

#### Path One — The Wanderers of Light
They believed light must be **followed**, not confined.
Truth lived in motion, stars, routes, songs, seasonal cycles, and living memory.
From this line emerged **Thiên Lữ**.

#### Path Two — The Keepers of Light
They believed light must be **guarded, interpreted, and centralized**.
Truth required sanctuaries, doctrine, sacred authority, and protected continuity.
From this line emerged the earliest form of **The Eliza tradition**.

### Canon Statement

> Thiên Lữ and The Eliza were once of one root.
> Their hatred is not merely political.
> It is the aftershock of a sacred fracture over what light was meant to be: a path to follow, or a truth to command.

## Era 3 — The Furnace and Rail Ascendancy

- Engineering polities expand.
- Ether-powered infrastructure becomes larger, harsher, and more systematic.
- The ancestors of the modern Republic Empire consolidate technical and political power.
- Linh Sơn communities retreat deeper into mountain and limestone strongholds, preserving a more spiritual relationship to Ether.
- Thiên Lữ formalizes its migratory civilization.
- Port ruins and salvage zones grow into the precursors of Scavenger society.

## Era 4 — The Fracture and Canon Wars

- Competing interpretations of history, Ether, sanctity, and legitimacy erupt into doctrine wars.
- The Eliza tradition undergoes reformations, schisms, purges, and reconsolidations.
- The cult around Golden Face evolves into full theological architecture.
- White stone sanctuaries, glass-lit theology, and purification doctrines become institutional.
- Regional powers fall, merge, or mythologize the past to justify survival.

## Era 5 — Present Convergence

- Ether shows signs of regional weakening.
- The outer edge of The Mist presses closer in unstable areas.
- Monster intrusions increase in frequency.
- Ruins become more active.
- Ancient records and half-buried truths start to matter again.
- Civilizations that once merely coexisted now enter a sharper age of collision.

# 8. Ether Weakening and the Current Crisis

This must be one of the major present-tense engines of the setting.

## 8.1 Canon Rule

Ether does **not** weaken uniformly.
It weakens:
- by region
- by cycle
- by extraction pressure
- by disruption of ancient Ether networks beneath the land

## 8.2 Consequences of Ether Weakening

When Ether density in a Stable Zone falls below critical thresholds:

### Phase 1 — Disturbance
- navigation anomalies increase
- dreams and visions intensify
- beasts become restless
- old devices malfunction unpredictably

### Phase 2 — Breach Signs
- local weather becomes wrong
- ruins awaken
- corrupted life appears in boundary regions
- The Mist seems closer even when coastlines look unchanged

### Phase 3 — Intrusion
- monsters from beyond the Mist enter more frequently
- buried entities from lower ruin-strata emerge
- reality tears, memory slips, and altered zones begin forming inside the continent

### Canon Statement

> Ether does not simply power civilization.
> It holds back the outside.
> When Ether weakens, the world does not go dark first.
> It begins to open.

# 9. The Eliza Across 2000 Years

The Eliza should not be treated as a single static church that somehow remained unchanged for two millennia.
Instead, it is a continuous evolving sacred tradition.

## Canon Model

The Eliza is the current name for a long-lasting theological lineage that persisted through many forms.
It has changed garments, doctrine, hierarchy, language, and sacred emphasis — but it never abandoned the central sanctification of Golden Face.

### Suggested Historical Stages

#### 1. The First Ash Cults
Survival communities near preserved shelters and luminous relic sites begin to mythologize the source of order in the ashes.

#### 2. The Lantern Canon
Early doctrine frames Golden Face as a guide through the age of ruin.

#### 3. The Ivory Synods
Structured priesthood, architecture, law, and orthodoxy form.

#### 4. The Scarlet Veil Reform
Purification, blood symbolism, judgment, and violent sanctification enter mainstream doctrine.

#### 5. The Present Eliza
A highly formalized divine-political tradition that sees itself as custodian of truth, order, and sacred continuity.

## Canon Truth

The Eliza is one of the oldest uninterrupted ideological currents in the world.
That makes it powerful — and also makes it one of the oldest lies still alive.

# 10. The Shared Root and Present Opposition of Thiên Lữ and The Eliza

This relationship should become one of the great emotional and symbolic axes of the world.

## Shared Ancestral Elements

Both cultures may preserve distorted fragments of the same original symbolic system:
- star motifs
- light circles
- veils and layered cloth
- sung ritual forms
- celestial diagrams
- sacred routes or sacred geometries
- memory transmission through repetition and pattern

## Divergence in Philosophy

### Thiên Lữ
- truth moves
- light is followed
- memory is carried
- revelation belongs to the road, the stars, and the living voice
- no one owns the sky

### The Eliza
- truth must be guarded
- light is interpreted through authority
- memory is purified into canon
- revelation must be protected from corruption
- sacred order requires center, hierarchy, and judgment

## Emotional Core

They do not merely disagree.
They are descendants of one broken inheritance.
Each sees the other not just as wrong, but as the one that betrayed the original meaning of the light.

# 11. Faction Truth Fragments

Each major civilization should hold only part of the truth.
No single faction knows enough to explain the world alone.

## Linh Sơn
Holds:
- truths of land, body, old Ether contact, and spiritual geography
- early inherited understanding that Ether is not just resource but living balance

Misunderstands:
- the full technological origin of Ether use

## Đế Quốc
Holds:
- engineering truth
- ruins knowledge
- technical continuity
- systemic understanding of civilization-scale Ether use

Misunderstands:
- the moral cost and hidden origins of the systems they inherited

## Thiên Lữ
Holds:
- mnemonic truth
- stellar cycles
- long-memory fragments encoded in songs and itinerant traditions

Misunderstands:
- material details of the old world

## The Eliza
Holds:
- geographical truth about Lady Ivan
- ritual continuity with early survival enclaves
- part of the oldest post-Collapse mythic archive

Misunderstands or suppresses:
- the human reality of Golden Face

## The Scavenger
Holds:
- material relic truth
- objects, scrap-tech, undocumented fragments, and black-market survival artifacts

Misunderstands:
- the meaning of many things they preserve and trade

# 12. Tone and Theme Lock

## Main Theme

> Humanity survives inside a wound that was mistaken for a new world.

## Secondary Theme

> History does not vanish.
> It mutates into religion, engineering, folklore, monsters, and borders.

## Donny Ivan Theme

> He is not a god, but history became so bent around his choices that later ages could no longer separate a man from the age he authored.

## Ether Theme

> Ether is not only power.
> It is the fragile condition that still allows human reality to hold.

## Thiên Lữ / Eliza Theme

> The road and the temple were once one tradition.
> Their war is a family wound stretched across centuries.

# 13. Immediate Story Value for Game Narrative

These revisions help answer the most important question for the playable present:

## Why does the story matter now?

Because now:
- Ether is weakening
- breaches are increasing
- monsters from beyond the Mist are appearing more often
- ancient ruins are becoming active
- old truths are becoming strategically dangerous
- factions with incompatible answers are forced into collision

This is what transforms the setting from background lore into active narrative pressure.

---

# 14. Final Canon Summary

WORLDSCOLIDE is a world 2000 years after a civilizational rupture caused by human ambition, technological discovery, and catastrophic strategic violence.

The current civilizations do not fully understand their own origin.
They survive within Ether-stabilized zones of reality while The Mist presses against the edges of the human world.

Donny Ivan did not create the world as a god.
But by breaking the old one and forcing the conditions of the new one, he became mythologized as something close to a creator.

Dr. Minh Yun discovered the light that changed everything.
Ivan bent that light into history.
The Eliza turned that history into doctrine.
Thiên Lữ carried its broken echo into the open sky.
And the world now stands at the edge of a new fracture as Ether weakens and the outside begins to enter.
