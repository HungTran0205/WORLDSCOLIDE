# WORLDSCOLIDE — Region Bible v1

> Internal worldbuilding document for writing, art direction, level design, quest design, and narrative consistency.
> 
> This document follows the revised canon established in **Canon Revision v2** and **Faction Bible v2**.

---

# 0. Design Purpose

This Region Bible is not only a map description. It is a **world operation manual** for regions.
Each region must function at the same time as:
- a physical place
- a cultural space
- a historical layer cake
- a gameplay zone
- a narrative pressure point
- a visual identity system

The world of WORLDSCOLIDE exists inside a fragile band of stability held together by **Ether**. Outside that band lies **The Mist** — not merely weather, but the damaged edge of reality where radiation scars, Etheric distortion, and alien ecologies overlap.

Because the new continent was formed from fractured remnants of the old world — pieces of Japan, the Americas, Indochina, and the Indonesian archipelago — its geography still carries **geological memory**. Mountains, faults, reefs, karst basins, peninsulas, and volcanic arcs remember what they once were, even if the people living there do not.

# 1. Global Geographic Principles

## 1.1 The Continent: Lost Angeles

**Lost Angeles** is the scholarly relic-name for the crescent-shaped super-continent formed after the Great Collapse. Most common people do not use that name. Depending on culture, it is called:
- **The Bent Land**
- **The Arc of Ashes**
- **The Held Continent**
- **The Surviving Crescent**

It is a jagged continental arc where:
- old mountain chains fused into a broken spine
- volcanic island belts welded into peninsulas and reef-choked coasts
- old river systems were interrupted, buried, or redirected
- entire shorelines were re-written by sea rise, tectonic violence, and Etheric stabilization

## 1.2 The Ether-Stability Belt

Human civilization only persists where Ether density remains strong enough to maintain **Zones of Stability**.
In such regions:
- weather remains legible
- direction can still be trusted most of the time
- ecosystems remain more coherent
- architecture can stand for generations
- memory and time are less likely to distort

Where Ether weakens:
- compasses fail
- migratory patterns break
- creatures from the Mist cross more easily
- ruins become “awake” or reactive
- local myths intensify because reality itself becomes less reliable

## 1.3 The Mist Rule of Three Layers

The Mist should always be treated as a three-layer phenomenon:

### Layer 1 — Physical Damage
Ash, toxic aerosols, magnetic interference, residue from the Black Material age, abnormal weather.

### Layer 2 — Etheric Distortion
Spatial confusion, misremembered routes, auditory mirages, memory echoes, unstable boundaries.

### Layer 3 — Altered Ecology
Mutated sea life, border predators, burrowing horrors, scavenger organisms, post-human survivals, and entities whose biology no longer matches old-world logic.

## 1.4 Regional Writing Rule

For any region in WORLDSCOLIDE, writers and designers should always answer six questions:
1. Why can people live there?
2. Why is it worth defending or conquering?
3. What old-world geography still echoes inside it?
4. How does Ether behave there?
5. What enters from below, from the border, or from the Mist?
6. What false belief about the place is currently accepted as truth?

# 3. LADY IVAN HOLY TERRITORIES

## 3.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** Sacred capital belt / theological state / buried secret city

**Primary Faction Influence:** The Eliza

**Core Fantasy:**
A radiant holy land of white stone, red veils, stained glass, choirs, processions, and immaculate ritual order — built atop a buried mouth into the deepest lies of history.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- awe
- discomfort
- reverence mixed with suspicion
- the sense that beauty here is being used as a mask

**Macro Role in the World:**
Lady Ivan is the ideological heart of the Eliza tradition and the longest surviving sacred claim to legitimacy after the Great Collapse. It is both pilgrimage center and propaganda engine.

## 3.2 Geological Memory

This region inherits memory from an old-world **peninsula and low-elevation coastal platform**, likely tied to the Florida-side of the old world. The land is relatively flatter than surrounding regions, but unstable beneath the surface:
- limestone shelves
- sink-prone substrata
- old buried infrastructure vaults
- saline winds and bright coastal light

The surface feels pure, but underground it is riddled with cavities, hidden chambers, forgotten flood tunnels, and sealed bunker arteries.

## 3.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- Ether concentration remains unusually stable due to ancient buried systems below the holy districts
- access to coast, salt, fisheries, and trade routes
- fertile belts in the inland terraces
- massive sacred labor infrastructure built over centuries

Why it matters strategically:
- spiritual control over millions of believers
- access to old-world underground vaults
- strong ceremonial roads and fortified monasteries
- coastal access to forbidden routes and relic smuggling

## 3.4 Settlements and Spatial Types

### The White Crown
The central sacred city, often simply called **Lady Ivan**. A city of elevated basilicas, processional roads, bell towers, red silk banners, polished plazas, and sealed sanctums.

### The Ivory Parishes
Rural temple-towns that produce grain, wine-like ceremonial drinks, medicinal herbs, paper, incense, and labor for the sanctified capital.

### The Pilgrim Steps
Road networks lined with shrines, ossuaries, candle stations, relic markets, and confession houses.

### The Underchapels
Catacomb layers used for archives, punishments, forbidden study, and tomb-ritual containment.

### The Sealed Foundations
Restricted excavation zones above old bunker layers. The upper clergy controls all access.

## 3.5 Civilian Life

Ordinary life in Lady Ivan is orderly, repetitive, and suffused with ritual.

People here:
- begin the day with light-facing prayer
- mark time by bells, processions, and liturgical calendars
- wear fabrics whose color saturation signifies rank and purity expectation
- receive moral education before practical education
- are taught that disorder is the first symptom of spiritual rot

Daily staples include:
- bread or flat grain loaves
- preserved fruits
- salted fish
- pale broths
- ceremonial oils
- white root vegetables cultivated in terraced inland gardens

Children are taught:
- posture before doctrine
- doctrine before doubt
- memory through recitation rather than interpretation

Common fears:
- contamination
- heresy accusations
- unexplained dreams
- speaking the wrong name in the wrong chapel
- being summoned below the city

## 3.6 Ether Relationship

The Eliza interpret Ether not as raw energy but as **sanctified illumination**.
Official doctrine claims:
- Ether is the mercy of Golden Face
- stable zones are proof of divine favor
- Ether disturbances indicate sin, impurity, or cosmic trial

The deeper truth:
- the region’s strongest Ether stability likely comes from buried old-world systems linked to Ivan’s bunker network
- rituals accidentally preserve ancient maintenance patterns
- certain sacred chants may be degraded operational sequences passed through religion

## 3.7 Threat Ecology

### Surface Threats
- doctrinal purges
- internal spies
- militant zealots
- procession riots triggered by miracles or rumors

### Subsurface Threats
- sealed bunker fauna
- ruin-reactive guardians
- gas pockets and sink collapses
- sound-sensitive creatures nesting in underchapel vaults

### Border Threats
- Mist-borne infiltrators near weakened coastal shrines
- relic smugglers using dead canals
- star-guides from Thiên Lữ seeking lost records

## 3.8 Historical Layers

### Layer I — Old World
A strategic bunker and elite survival infrastructure tied to the Ivan era.

### Layer II — Early Ash Cults
The earliest survivors around the bunker begin interpreting protected light, ventilation, machines, and sealed chambers as signs of divine intervention.

### Layer III — Sacred Consolidation
The proto-Eliza faith develops liturgy, hierarchy, relic law, and monumental architecture.

### Layer IV — Canon Wars
Competing sects battle over the meaning of Golden Face, purification doctrine, and access to underground truths.

### Layer V — Present Day
Lady Ivan appears eternal, but its underground truths are becoming harder to keep buried as Ether weakens.

## 3.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** Lady Ivan was blessed from the beginning and chosen as the resting place of sacred light.

**The clergy teach:** Golden Face created the current order and entrusted the Eliza to protect it.

**Closer truth:** Lady Ivan sits on one of the most important surviving old-world underground systems, and its holiness grew around a misunderstanding that later became power.

## 3.10 Visual Direction

**Color palette:** ivory white, blood red, muted gold, candle amber, pale stone gray

**Shapes:** arches, vertical spires, veiled silhouettes, fan-like windows, descending stair geometry

**Materials:** polished limestone, white plaster, stained glass, aged bronze, silk, ash-lacquered wood

**Light language:** beautiful, directional, oppressive; sacred light should feel too deliberate

## 3.11 Key Landmarks

### The Basilica of the Veiled Face
Largest sacred structure in the region. Houses the most revered icon of Golden Face.

### The Red Processional Spine
A ceremonial avenue where state theology is performed as public spectacle.

### The Bell of First Mercy
Ancient bell said to ring differently when Ether density changes.

### The Lower Mercy Vaults
Supposed burial sites of saints; in truth, they may be interfaces into deeper pre-collapse architecture.

### Saint Elara’s Sink
A holy collapse pit regarded as miraculous punishment; possibly an exposed fracture into older chambers.

## 3.12 Quest Hooks

- Recover a forbidden hymn whose melody matches a bunker unlock pattern.
- Investigate why bells across three districts ring at unscheduled hours during Mist surges.
- Escort a reformist archivist who believes Golden Face was a man, not a god.
- Discover why children born in a particular parish keep drawing the same underground chamber.

# 4. LINH SƠN KARST REALM

## 4.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** Karst mountain civilization / ancestral highlands / Ether-root sanctuary

**Primary Faction Influence:** Linh Sơn

**Core Fantasy:**
A realm of limestone towers, hanging settlements, cave shrines, hidden valleys, mist-fed forests, and ancestral lineages who can still feel the pulse of Ether moving through stone and bone.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- grounded wonder
- endurance
- intimacy with stone, water, and memory
- a sense that the land itself is listening

**Macro Role in the World:**
Linh Sơn is one of the oldest surviving cultures to preserve a non-industrial yet highly sophisticated relationship with Ether. They are custodians of balance, but also keepers of secrets they may have hidden for too long.

## 4.2 Geological Memory

This region inherits heavily from old-world **Indochinese karst systems**:
- limestone towers
- cave rivers
- sink valleys
- hanging cliffs
- mineral springs
- hidden plateaus

It is a land of vertical complexity. Travel here is not only horizontal; it is ascended, descended, lowered, ferried, and remembered through the body.

## 4.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- abundant spring water
- hidden farmlands in valleys and shelf terraces
- caves that stabilize temperature and weather extremes
- defensive terrain
- stable Ether veins aligned with deep geological structure

Why it matters strategically:
- natural fortification
- sacred Ether nodes
- hidden passes invisible to outsiders
- access to ancient stone-hewn ruin chambers untouched by imperial roads

## 4.4 Settlements and Spatial Types

### Cliff Halls
Major mountain settlements built into limestone faces with timber platforms, rope bridges, drum towers, and ancestral altars.

### Root Valleys
Agricultural basin communities fed by springs, cave runoff, and terraced wet fields.

### Shrine Caves
Ceremonial chambers where elders read resonance patterns in stone, flame, and body vibration.

### Spirit Forges
Sites where salvaged ancient metal and mountain ores are smelted under ritual supervision.

### Silent Passes
Border corridors defended not by walls, but by geography, memory, and traps hidden in terrain.

## 4.5 Civilian Life

Life in Linh Sơn is communal, labor-centered, and ancestral.

People here:
- farm on terraces and valley floors
- train the body from childhood as a moral as much as practical discipline
- maintain household altars tied to clan lines and mountain guardians
- think in terms of bloodline, watershed, and debt to land

Common foods include:
- mountain rice or grain
- root vegetables
- smoked meats
- fermented forest greens
- river fish
- mineral-rich herbal broths

Children are taught:
- how to read weather on stone surfaces
- which springs can be trusted
- which songs must be sung in caves
- what not to dig, speak, or disturb

Common fears:
- cracking a sacred vein in the mountain
- angering ancestral grounds
- outsiders taking Ether-rich stone without understanding its cost
- the day the mountain stops answering back

## 4.6 Ether Relationship

For Linh Sơn, Ether is not abstract power. It is **long-mạch**, the living current of land-memory.

They believe:
- Ether moves through mountains the way blood moves through living bodies
- human life and mountain stability mirror one another
- extraction without ritual balance invites collapse, blight, or madness

Possible deeper truth:
- Linh Sơn traditions preserve a pre-scientific but highly accurate understanding of regional Ether flow
- some elder rituals may encode field measurements in mythic language

## 4.7 Threat Ecology

### Natural Threats
- landslides
- sink collapses
- flood surges through cave channels
- spore zones in sealed caverns

### Creature Threats
- cliff leapers
- blind cave burrowers
- root-parasitic stone feeders
- Mistborne things entering through deep subterranean cracks

### Human Threats
- imperial survey teams
- relic poachers
- border zealots accusing mountain rites of heresy

## 4.8 Historical Layers

### Layer I — Old World Geology
Karst systems, tropical mountain belts, and limestone aquifers from the Indochina memory strata.

### Layer II — Early Ether Contact
One of the first societies to interact closely with raw Ether-bearing zones after the Collapse.

### Layer III — Ancestral Consolidation
Clans, lineages, cave rites, and body-marking traditions develop around balance and survival.

### Layer IV — Wars of Extraction
Repeated attempts by outside powers to map, harvest, or annex Linh Sơn Ether nodes.

### Layer V — Present Day
The realm remains resilient, but its elders know the resonance of the mountains has begun to thin.

## 4.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** the mountains are eternal if properly respected.

**The elders teach:** only correct ritual balance keeps stone, body, and spirit aligned.

**Closer truth:** Linh Sơn has preserved precious knowledge about Ether balance, but their secrecy may have prevented the wider world from understanding the scale of the current collapse.

## 4.10 Visual Direction

**Color palette:** wet stone gray, moss green, aged bronze, indigo cloth, earth brown, ancestral fire orange

**Shapes:** steep diagonals, layered terraces, suspended bridges, drum circles, hanging roots

**Materials:** limestone, bamboo, hardwood, leather, fiber cloth, patinated bronze, ritual lacquer

**Light language:** filtered, humid, alive; light should feel caught by leaves, mist, and stone edges

## 4.11 Key Landmarks

### Hall of Nine Echoes
An ancestral chamber where stone resonance is read as historical memory.

### The Hanging Root Fortress
A defensive settlement wrapped around giant tree roots growing from cliff faces.

### The White Vein Chasm
A sacred Ether-bearing mineral split in the mountain that no one is allowed to mine directly.

### Drumbridge of Khun Vân
A bridge used for ceremonies, alarms, and oath-making.

### The Sleeping Throat
A cave sink long believed sealed, now emitting strange warm fog and low-frequency hums.

## 4.12 Quest Hooks

- Track a border survey team that vanished while mapping forbidden caverns.
- Stop an elder faction from sealing a valley whose people need outside help.
- Investigate a newborn generation whose body marks glow without ritual tattooing.
- Descend into the Sleeping Throat where Mist creatures may be entering from below instead of from the sea.

# 5. THE REPUBLIC RAIL BASTIONS

## 5.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** Industrial corridor state / rail empire spine / managed Ether infrastructure zone

**Primary Faction Influence:** The Republic Empire

**Core Fantasy:**
Cities of steel-glass, pressure towers, humming substations, rail fortresses, and planned districts where the descendants of engineered civilization try to hold chaos back with discipline, calculation, and networked force.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- admiration for scale and efficiency
- unease at over-control
- the feeling of civilization fighting entropy by command and machine

**Macro Role in the World:**
The Rail Bastions are the most systematized region on the continent. They are proof that order can still be built — and a warning that order can become extraction disguised as necessity.

## 5.2 Geological Memory

This region inherits from fused old-world **continental corridors, river-fed industrial plains, and mountain-foot resource belts**. Its geology supports:
- rail lines across broad flats
- fortified river crossings
- resource extraction at the edge of uplands
- enormous freight basins

It is one of the best places on the continent for infrastructure to scale.

## 5.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- managed water systems
- agricultural belts tied to logistics networks
- electrified and steam-driven industrial support
- heavy Ether regulation via state-controlled grids

Why it matters strategically:
- troop movement
- manufacturing
- archive preservation
- long-range coordination
- access to relic refinement and mechanical production

## 5.4 Settlements and Spatial Types

### Rail Citadels
Massive fortified urban centers built around stations, foundries, administrative towers, and Ether substations.

### Line Towns
Settlements that exist to service rail routes, bridgeworks, inspection towers, and engine depots.

### Furnace Districts
High-heat industrial zones where metal, glass, ceramics, and machine components are produced.

### Archive Wards
Restricted educational and technical quarters where old diagrams, translated manuals, and doctrinal engineering texts are kept.

### Survey Frontiers
Expansion belts at the edge of known stable territory.

## 5.5 Civilian Life

Daily life is punctual, regulated, and administratively legible.

People here:
- live by schedules and permits
- identify themselves by district, trade, and certification level
- see infrastructure maintenance as civic morality
- are raised to believe that disorder kills faster than cruelty

Common foods include:
- preserved grains
- hearty soups
- processed root cakes
- workshop breads
- ration stews
- tea-like stimulant infusions

Children are taught:
- counting, measurement, and state history early
- safety procedures before philosophy
- respect for chain of command
- pride in technical continuity with the age before oblivion

Common fears:
- system failure
- rail sabotage
- blackouts
- unexplained anomalies in controlled districts
- discovering that the state is running out of Ether faster than it admits

## 5.6 Ether Relationship

The Republic treats Ether as:
- infrastructure
- strategic necessity
- measurable field phenomenon
- the backbone of modern order

Official doctrine:
- Ether must be managed scientifically
- civilization survives only through organized extraction and distribution

Deeper truth:
- their control systems are brilliant but incomplete
- many operating principles derive from fragmented old-world knowledge whose original context has been lost
- the very scale of their Ether use may be accelerating regional instability

## 5.7 Threat Ecology

### Structural Threats
- pressure failures
- arc surges
- sabotage cells
- rogue engineering cults

### Border Threats
- Mist incursions along weak freight routes
- mobile raider strikes from fractured zones
- star-guides interfering with survey claims

### Internal Threats
- labor unrest
- technocratic factionalism
- archive suppression
- political purges disguised as safety reform

## 5.8 Historical Layers

### Layer I — Old World Industrial Memory
River corridors, transport logic, and resource concentration patterns survive in geography.

### Layer II — Ether Reconstruction Period
The earliest post-collapse technical lineages begin reconstructing large-scale systems.

### Layer III — Imperial Standardization
The descendants of this tradition build the frameworks that will become the Republic.

### Layer IV — Expansion and Rational Dominion
Survey, extraction, and administrative integration expand outward.

### Layer V — Present Day
The Bastions are stronger than ever on the surface, but the cost of maintaining order is mounting.

## 5.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** the Republic is the most rational guardian of human survival.

**The state teaches:** without disciplined Ether management, the continent would already have fallen.

**Closer truth:** the Republic is both indispensable and dangerous; it may be holding the world together while also helping drain the reserves that keep it stable.

## 5.10 Visual Direction

**Color palette:** steel gray, soot black, electric blue, brass gold, furnace orange, paper white

**Shapes:** strong verticals, rail lines, repeating windows, disciplined geometry, pressure-cylinder silhouettes

**Materials:** steel, glass, rivets, cables, ceramic insulators, painted enamel, polished brass

**Light language:** engineered, practical, controlled; bright only where function demands it

## 5.11 Key Landmarks

### The Hundred-Line Terminus
Central transit-fortress where rail networks converge.

### Bastion Meridian Tower
Administrative and signal command center overseeing line security and field reports.

### The Glass Senate
Seat of high governance, acoustically designed to make all speech feel official.

### Foundry Ring Theta
A legendary industrial district where battlefield hardware and civic systems are both produced.

### The Silent Reservoir
A restricted Ether storage complex where unexplained losses have begun to appear in the logs.

## 5.12 Quest Hooks

- Audit a regional Ether loss report that points toward impossible consumption rates.
- Smuggle an old technical codex out of an archive before it is classified as destabilizing.
- Investigate why one line town insists its rails “sing” before Mist incursions.
- Choose between defending a vital extraction route or shutting it down to preserve a failing Ether node.

# 6. THE STAR-STEPPES OF THIÊN LỮ

## 6.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** Nomadic sky-country / caravan belt / memory ecology

**Primary Faction Influence:** Thiên Lữ

**Core Fantasy:**
An immense belt of high grasslands, salt basins, cold night plateaus, luminous cloth cities, and moving societies who navigate not by borders but by stars, songs, migration cycles, and signs most settled peoples no longer know how to read.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- freedom
- melancholy
- wonder at scale
- intimacy with sky and motion
- the sense that memory can travel without walls

**Macro Role in the World:**
Thiên Lữ territory prevents the world from being fully pinned down. It is a living archive on the move, and one of the last cultural systems that still understands cyclical time instead of purely administrative time.

## 6.2 Geological Memory

This region carries memory from **highland plateaus, rain-shadow steppes, dry basins, volcanic uplifts, and migratory corridors**. Influences from old-world America, volcanic arcs, and altered island uplands create a terrain of:
- open horizons
- harsh winds
- reflective salt flats
- crater lakes
- isolated standing stones
- seasonal grass oceans

## 6.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- mobility rather than fixed density
- knowledge of seasonal water and grazing routes
- portable architecture adapted to shifting conditions
- strong cultural memory of star and wind navigation

Why it matters strategically:
- it is a corridor linking multiple civilizations
- only Thiên Lữ can cross some regions safely year-round
- many hidden relic fields lie beneath the steppe belts
- old sky-aligned sites may interact with Ether cycles

## 6.4 Settlements and Spatial Types

### Walking Cities
Massive caravan formations of wagons, beast-drawn platforms, tethered cloth towers, shrine-carts, food caravans, and archive tents.

### Star Camps
Temporary circular camps laid out according to sky geometry and season.

### Salt Mirrors
Shallow basin sites used for reflection rites, prophecy, and navigation training.

### Song Posts
Marker pillars where encoded verses and route warnings are preserved.

### Horizon Graves
Ancient burial circles where certain lineages leave memory objects for future migrations.

## 6.5 Civilian Life

Life here is mobile, communal, and mnemonic.

People here:
- own less weight, but more stories
- treat movement as continuity, not instability
- raise children across many hands and many fires
- preserve law through song, knotwork, tattoos, sky diagrams, and repeated oral patterns

Common foods include:
- dried meats
- grain cakes
- fermented milk or herb drinks
- smoked roots
- foraged salts
- mobile hearth stews

Children are taught:
- star positions before borders
- route songs before genealogy charts
- how to recognize “wrong wind” nights
- that memory is a duty, not just inheritance

Common fears:
- losing the rhythm of migration
- a song line being forgotten
- false skies caused by Ether distortion
- settling too long in a place that wants to trap them

## 6.6 Ether Relationship

Thiên Lữ do not think of Ether as ownership-worthy substance. To them, Ether is a **current of signs**.

They believe:
- Ether changes how sky, land, and memory answer one another
- there are good routes and dead routes depending on Ether cycles
- some songs must be sung at exact stars or the world stops answering correctly

Deeper truth:
- Thiên Lữ preserve old interpretive systems that may partially encode real astronomical and Etheric forecasting
- their shared origin with the proto-Eliza tradition survives in symbols, liturgical structure, and ritual timing, though they interpret it very differently

## 6.7 Threat Ecology

### Environmental Threats
- sudden cold fronts
- dry lightning fields
- false horizons
- sink salts
- night predators using sound reflection

### Mist-Border Threats
- long-limbed trackers emerging from weak-steppe edges
- sky-maddened flocks
- echo beasts drawn to sung patterns

### Human Threats
- Eliza purges
- imperial corridor claims
- bandit confederacies that mimic caravan signals

## 6.8 Historical Layers

### Layer I — Old World Horizon Lands
Plateaus, uplands, volcanic basins, and migratory corridors.

### Layer II — Shared Light Tradition
Before full division, precursor groups to Thiên Lữ and Eliza belonged to a tradition that interpreted Ether through sky, memory, and ritual order.

### Layer III — The Great Schism of the Open Path
A doctrinal fracture leads one branch toward traveling revelation and the other toward fixed sacred authority.

### Layer IV — Age of Caravans
Thiên Lữ society matures into moving cultural polities with song-law and star-guided migration.

### Layer V — Present Day
They still move, but the skies have become harder to trust, and the songs are warning them that something old is approaching.

## 6.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** the stars remember what the earth has forgotten.

**Thiên Lữ elders teach:** movement preserves truth better than monuments do.

**Closer truth:** Thiên Lữ do preserve fragments of world truth — but in forms so encoded and dispersed that even they no longer fully understand all of what they carry.

## 6.10 Visual Direction

**Color palette:** night blue, dust violet, silver ash, pale turquoise, moon white, ember gold

**Shapes:** curved tents, trailing banners, wheel forms, horn silhouettes, layered cloth arcs, circular camp layouts

**Materials:** woven luminous fibers, bone, bronze, weathered leather, salt-glass beads, polished stone, hide parchment

**Light language:** low, moving, intimate by fire — infinite and cold under stars

## 6.11 Key Landmarks

### The Seven Reflection Basin
A ritual salt mirror where sky readings are conducted at major seasonal crossings.

### The Archive Caravan of Half-Voices
A legendary moving archive whose songs are missing crucial verses.

### The Open Path Scar
A geological rift associated with the ancient schism between traveling and fixed interpretations of light.

### The Silent Constellation Stones
Standing stones that align to stars no longer visible every year.

### Windmother Hollow
A crater-lake route marker said to echo back names spoken by the dead.

## 6.12 Quest Hooks

- Recover a stolen verse that changes the meaning of an entire migration song.
- Escort a child who can identify “false stars” appearing over Ether-thinned regions.
- Investigate whether a sacred Eliza rite and a Thiên Lữ sky-song are actually two halves of the same ancient operating sequence.
- Trace a vanished walking city whose route ends at the edge of the Mist with no return trail.

# 7. THE RUST PORTS OF SCAVENGER

## 7.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** Ruin-harbor confederacy / salvage economy / law-by-edge zone

**Primary Faction Influence:** The Scavenger

**Core Fantasy:**
A chain of toxic harbors, broken drydocks, rust citadels, knife-law markets, ship graveyards, masked guilds, and violence disciplined not by virtue, but by contract, debt, and reputation among those who learned to survive on the corpse of industry.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- danger
- excitement
- grime and ingenuity
- the feeling that everything has a price and none of it is clean

**Macro Role in the World:**
The Rust Ports are the continent’s black lungs and black market. They are where old-world metal resurfaces, where forbidden trade crosses faction lines, and where practical knowledge survives in ugly forms respectable nations pretend not to need.

## 7.2 Geological Memory

This region inherits from **old harbors, industrial coasts, island channels, storm-worn ship corridors, and tectonically damaged maritime choke points**. The coast is a tangle of:
- drowned piers
- rusting hull fields
- reefed channels
- black tidal flats
- jagged inlets
- toxic marsh belts

## 7.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- access to salvage, fish, trade, and smuggling routes
- high adaptability and low cultural dependence on purity
- mastery of reclaimed materials and improvised infrastructure
- port settlements built on layered wreckage and elevated gangways

Why it matters strategically:
- relic trade
- scrap metal, machine parts, strange fuels, contraband Ether tech
- access to dangerous sea routes no one else will maintain
- intelligence exchange across enemy lines

## 7.4 Settlements and Spatial Types

### Wreck-Citadels
Fortified port cities built from ship skeletons, steel towers, crane bones, and layered dockworks.

### Knife Markets
Trade hubs where all law is transactional and armed.

### Mask Yards
Craft districts producing armor, mempo masks, forged blades, salvage tools, and coded guild insignia.

### Chain Slips
Raised tidal passageways connecting neighborhoods above poisonous flats.

### Dead Anchor Fields
Ship grave zones used for relic diving, ambushes, duels, and illegal rites.

## 7.5 Civilian Life

Life here is harsh, improvisational, and unsentimental.

People here:
- value practical loyalty over ideology
- identify by guild, crew, family debt, or market contract
- distrust purity and grand speeches
- often grow up learning trade and violence at the same time

Common foods include:
- dried fish
- seaweed stews
- shell meats
- smoked port grains
- fungus bread
- brined roots grown in hard coastal soils

Children are taught:
- how to judge metal quality by sound
- which currents drag bodies and which drag treasure
- how to read debt marks and guild colors
- never to draw a blade without understanding the contract behind it

Common fears:
- losing face publicly
- owing the wrong house
- breathing the wrong fog
- dredging something that should have remained underwater

## 7.6 Ether Relationship

Scavenger culture treats Ether with brutal pragmatism.

For them Ether is:
- fuel
- risk
- leverage
- explosive opportunity

Unlike the Republic, they do not believe Ether can be perfectly controlled.
Unlike Eliza, they do not sanctify it.
Unlike Linh Sơn, they do not trust balance.
Unlike Thiên Lữ, they do not read its signs gently.

They harvest, jury-rig, weaponize, barter, or fear it depending on the moment.

## 7.7 Threat Ecology

### Environmental Threats
- toxic tide mists
- collapsing dock layers
- razor reefs
- hull parasites
- drowned machine nests

### Creature Threats
- reef crawlers
- oil-black leviathan spawn
- undertide predators nesting in wrecks
- Mist-sea organisms adapted to saline toxicity

### Human Threats
- guild feuds
- contract killings
- masked ronin orders
- false neutral brokers

## 7.8 Historical Layers

### Layer I — Old World Ports
Industrial maritime networks, fuel transport, ship grave infrastructure.

### Layer II — Toxic Survival Era
Harbor survivors adapt to poisoned coasts and wreck economies.

### Layer III — Contract Houses Rise
Trade law, dueling law, and masked authority stabilize chaos without civilizing it.

### Layer IV — Confederated Ruthlessness
Multiple ports connect by profit and fear, not true unity.

### Layer V — Present Day
Scavenger ports thrive while the sea grows stranger and the Mist presses harder inland.

## 7.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** Scavenger territory is pure chaos.

**Outsiders teach:** it is a nest of degenerates and raiders.

**Closer truth:** the Rust Ports are brutally ordered by a code outsiders do not understand. Their society is unstable, but not formless.

## 7.10 Visual Direction

**Color palette:** rust black, toxic green, burned orange, jade edge, sea-gray, salt white

**Shapes:** cranes, hooks, blade lines, layered gangways, hull ribs, mask silhouettes

**Materials:** rusted steel, tire rubber, rope, reclaimed wood, patched cloth, ceramic seals, salt-caked metal

**Light language:** flare sparks, toxic fog glow, lantern reflections on wet iron, knife-edge contrast

## 7.11 Key Landmarks

### Port Kagemaw
The largest contract port and symbolic heart of Scavenger prestige.

### The Iron Grave Tideyard
A tidal wreck field where valuable relic dives border on suicide.

### House of Split Blades
A dueling court where contractual violence is formalized.

### The Deep Ledger Vault
A sealed market archive recording debts old enough to reshape alliances.

### Mempo Reef
A coastal grave chain where masks of the dishonored are nailed into coral-metal outgrowths.

## 7.12 Quest Hooks

- Recover a relic cargo before rival houses turn its auction into a massacre.
- Investigate a guild that is smuggling live Mist organisms as weapons.
- Clear a Dead Anchor Field where a submerged old-world fuel complex is reacting to Ether leakage.
- Win or disrupt a formal blade-contract duel whose outcome will determine port access for another faction.

# 8. THE SHATTERED MARCHES

## 8.1 Region Identity

**Region Type:** contested frontier belt / scarred borderland / campaign country

**Primary Faction Influence:** mixed — Republic, Linh Sơn, Eliza, and Scavenger proxies all operate here; Thiên Lữ crosses through seasonally

**Core Fantasy:**
A land of ruined forts, abandoned villages, cracked roads, half-functioning towers, old trenches, broken shrines, and shifting lines of control where no story remains pure and every faction leaves its dead in the same mud.

**Player Emotion Goals:**
- tension
- instability
- exhaustion
- moral ambiguity

**Macro Role in the World:**
The Marches are the place where the grand claims of civilizations become practical violence. They are essential to showing the cost of ideology on ordinary land.

## 8.2 Geological Memory

The Shattered Marches inherit from multiple fused old-world edges:
- broken foothills
- floodplains interrupted by uplift
- former transport crossings
- dry belts collapsing into marsh belts

This is a region where geography was never fully re-stabilized after the Collapse.

## 8.3 Geographic Logic

Why people can live here:
- fragmented pockets of fertile land
- access routes between major civilizations
- scavenged fortifications and old roads

Why it matters strategically:
- corridor access
- resource transit
- military staging
- propaganda value of border control

## 8.4 Settlement Types

- fortified market villages
- militia towers
- refugee camps turned permanent towns
- half-abandoned shrines
- repurposed survey forts
- burial fields reclaimed as farmland

## 8.5 Civilian Life

People here live in contingency.
Everything is provisional:
- loyalties
- walls
- crop plans
- worship practices
- even regional names

Children in the Marches learn to:
- identify banners quickly
- hide valuables in multiple locations
- recognize whether a passing patrol is hungry, holy, or desperate

## 8.6 Ether Relationship

The region is patchy.
Strong and weak Ether zones alternate unpredictably, making the Marches ideal for:
- ambushes
- strange weather pockets
- ruin activation events
- local miracles and local catastrophes

## 8.7 Threat Ecology

- patrol conflict
- displaced monsters
- sudden Mist breaches through weak folds
- grave-field anomalies
- relic-triggered violence

## 8.8 Historical Layers

The Marches contain the sediment of many wars and campaigns. In some places five different emblems can be found on the same collapsed wall.

## 8.9 Truth / Falsehood Layer

**People believe:** one day one side will finally secure lasting control.

**Closer truth:** the Marches exist precisely because no single truth, army, or doctrine can digest them for long.

## 8.10 Visual Direction

Broken banners, mud walls, burnt grain fields, fractured roads, recycled stonework, multi-faith graffiti, temporary shrines.

## 8.11 Key Landmarks

- The Broken Meridian Fort
- Fields of Seven Standards
- The Hollow Survey Road
- Saint-and-Serpent Bridge
- The Refuge Bell Tower

## 8.12 Quest Hooks

- broker a truce over a village sitting above a weak Ether seam
- expose which faction deliberately caused a Mist breach to justify occupation
- escort refugees whose oral history preserves a lost battle truth
- uncover a grave site where soldiers from enemy sides were buried together by survivors

# 12. Tone Guardrails for Region Writing

When writing regions for WORLDSCOLIDE:

## Always preserve
- beauty mixed with unease
- the sense of long history pressed into land
- conflict between belief and truth
- grounded survival logic beneath mythic atmosphere
- the idea that Ether keeps more than lights running — it keeps human reality coherent

## Avoid
- making any region morally simple
- making any civilization fully correct
- turning The Mist into generic fog
- flattening geography into “biome only” thinking
- over-explaining every mystery too early

## Narrative Principle
A region in WORLDSCOLIDE should feel like a place where:
- people have survived for centuries
- history has been misunderstood repeatedly
- something under the ground or beyond the border is starting to move again

# 14. Closing Statement

WORLDSCOLIDE’s regions should never feel like disconnected zones.

Each region is a way human beings tried to remain human:
- by sanctifying light
- by listening to stone
