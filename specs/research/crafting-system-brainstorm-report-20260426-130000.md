# Crafting System Brainstorm for Worlds Collide

**Date:** 2026-04-26 13:00:00  
**Researcher:** tm.researcher  
**Topic:** Balanced crafting system using blanks and monster drops for up to 4 stat slots on weapons/armor, with tiered progression, stat ranges, fail rates, and idle RPG balance.

## Executive Summary

This brainstorm proposes a crafting system for Worlds Collide where players craft blanks from wood/ore and combine them with monster drops to add stat lines to gear. The system features 4 slots with tiered progression (common/basic for slot 1, powerful/mid for slots 2-3, legendary for slot 4), defined min-max ranges for each stat, increasing fail rates (20-50% destruction risk), and stat placement suggestions to prevent power creep. Balance ensures idle gameplay remains viable by requiring time-gated resources and avoiding overpowered combinations.

## Crafting Mechanics Overview

- **Blanks**: Crafted from resources (wood for weapons, ore for armor). Base cost scales with gear level.
- **Monster Drops**: Essences/gems providing stat templates. Rarity determines stat tier potential.
- **Slots**: Up to 4 stat lines per item. Each slot unlocked sequentially with increasing risk.
- **Process**: Combine blank + drop in crafting station. Success adds stat; failure destroys item (increasing risk per slot).
- **Idle Balance**: Crafting requires offline farming time for drops/resources. Stats enhance idle efficiency but don't eliminate grinding.

## Stat Categorization and Slot Progression

Stats categorized into tiers based on RPG balancing research (Diablo 3, WoW, PoE). Slot 1: common/basic for early viability. Slots 2-3: powerful/mid for progression. Slot 4: legendary for end-game challenge.

### Slot 1: Common/Basic
Suitable for early slots to build foundational power without creep. Linear scaling, essential for survival/utility.

- Threat: 5-50 (aggro generation)
- Defense Rating: 10-200 (damage reduction)
- HP: 50-500 (health pool)
- Mana: 20-200 (resource pool)
- Mana Regen: 1-10/sec (sustained recovery)
- Accuracy: 1%-10% (hit chance)
- AS (Attack Speed): 5%-15% (action rate)
- Dodge: 1%-5% (evasion chance)
- Duration: 10%-50% (effect length)
- Chance: 5%-20% (proc probability)

### Slots 2-3: Powerful/Mid-Tier
Mid-game progression stats with multiplicative effects. Balanced for power without trivializing content.

- Damage Melee: 10-100 (flat physical damage)
- Crit Rate: 5%-20% (critical hit chance)
- Crit Dam: 50%-150% (critical multiplier)
- Effect Chance: 10%-30% (special effect probability)
- Damage Range: 10-100 (flat ranged damage)
- Magic Damage: 10-100 (flat elemental damage)
- Element Chance: 5%-15% (elemental proc chance)
- Heal Amount: 20-200 (healing potency)
- Buff Duration: 20%-50% (beneficial effect extension)
- Shield: 50-500 (absorptive defense)
- Invi Duration: 5-30 sec (stealth uptime)

### Slot 4: Legendary/Overpowered
End-game stats with extreme scaling/caps. Reserved for legendary gear to prevent early access.

- Crit Dam: 200%-500% (extreme multipliers, cap at 500%)
- Effect Chance: 50%-100% (guaranteed procs)
- Heal Amount: 500-2000 (massive regen, diminishing returns)
- Buff Duration: 100%-200% (near-permanent buffs)
- Shield: 1000-5000 (high absorptive, cap at 50% max HP)
- Invi Duration: 60-300 sec (extended stealth)

## Fail Rates and Destruction Risk

Fail rates increase per slot to create tension and prevent spamming. Destruction risk aligns with researched 20-50% range, favoring skilled/timed play.

- Slot 1: 20% fail rate (low risk for basics)
- Slot 2: 30% fail rate (moderate risk)
- Slot 3: 40% fail rate (high risk)
- Slot 4: 50% fail rate (extreme risk, legendary stakes)

Failure destroys the item, requiring recrafting blank. Mitigation: Premium materials reduce risk by 10-20%.

## Stat Placement for Power Creep Prevention

Early slots focus on survival/utility to enable progression. Late slots introduce damage/output boosts to reward investment without front-loading power.

- **Early Slots (1-2)**: Defense Rating, HP, Mana, Dodge, Accuracy, Mana Regen, Threat, Duration, Chance. Builds tanky/sustainable builds.
- **Mid Slots (2-3)**: AS, Damage Melee/Range, Crit Rate, Effect Chance, Element Chance, Heal Amount, Buff Duration, Shield. Enhances offense/defense without extremes.
- **Late Slots (4)**: Crit Dam, Invi Duration. Game-changing but gated by high risk/failure.

Prevents creep by ensuring core power comes from leveling/gear base, not crafting alone.

## Idle RPG Balance Considerations

- **Time-Gated Resources**: Monster drops require offline battle timers (e.g., 1-4 hours per rare drop).
- **Resource Scarcity**: Blanks consume rare wood/ore farmed via idle production.
- **Diminishing Returns**: Multiple same stats on gear reduce effectiveness (e.g., 2x HP mods give 1.5x total).
- **Synergy Limits**: Crafting encourages diverse builds; over-specialization penalized by idle inefficiencies.
- **Progression Gates**: Higher slots unlock at gear levels, ensuring idle grinding remains core loop.
